How many public bins are in Gothenburg?

Gothenburg has approximately 1564 public waste bins mapped on OpenStreetMap, including 8 dedicated dog waste bins. The city averages 7.8 bins per square kilometer. bin there shows all these bins on an interactive map with walking directions to the nearest one, so you never have to search for a place to dispose of waste.
