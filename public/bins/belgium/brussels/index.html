How many public bins are in Brussels?

Brussels has approximately 6915 public waste bins mapped on OpenStreetMap, including 11 dedicated dog waste bins. The city averages 34.4 bins per square kilometer. bin there shows all these bins on an interactive map with walking directions to the nearest one, so you never have to search for a place to dispose of waste.
