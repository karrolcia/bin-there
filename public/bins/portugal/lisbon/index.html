How many public bins are in Lisbon?

Lisbon has approximately 520 public waste bins mapped on OpenStreetMap. The city averages 2.6 bins per square kilometer. bin there shows all these bins on an interactive map with walking directions to the nearest one, so you never have to search for a place to dispose of waste.
